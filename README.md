# Amal Engineering Company — Corporate Website

Official corporate website for **Amal Engineering Company**, an integrated engineering, construction, water well drilling, hydraulic infrastructure, and renewable solar power solutions provider headquartered in Djibouti with operations spanning **Djibouti, Ethiopia, and Somaliland**.

---

## 🏗️ Overview

This repository contains the complete responsive corporate website built according to Amal Engineering Company's corporate profile, technical capabilities, fleet inventory, and regional operations.

### Key Sections & Pages
- **Home (`index.html`)**: Executive summary, operational metrics counter, core service pillars, regional footprint, heavy machinery showcase, and field operations preview.
- **About Us (`about.html`)**: Company history, mission, vision, core values, technical leadership, and Horn of Africa presence.
- **Services Overview (`services.html`)**: End-to-end engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) solutions.
- **Water Well Drilling & Supply (`water-drilling.html`)**: Hydrogeological surveys, deep borehole rotary drilling, step-drawdown testing, and community distribution networks.
- **Construction & Infrastructure (`construction.html`)**: Reinforced concrete, commercial buildings, earthworks, foundations, and civil infrastructure.
- **Solar Energy Systems (`solar-energy.html`)**: Solar water pumping, commercial/industrial off-grid & hybrid solar installations, and microgrids.
- **General Services & Supplies (`general-services.html`)**: Machinery rental, equipment leasing, site logistics, and procurement.
- **Equipment & Fleet (`equipment.html`)**: Comprehensive inventory including 50-ton crawler crane, rotary rigs, excavators, tipper trucks, and auxiliary support units.
- **Safety & Sustainability (`safety.html`)**: Quality (ISO 9001), Environmental (ISO 14001), and Occupational Safety (ISO 45001) management frameworks.
- **Field Operations (`projects.html`)**: Interactive categorized operational gallery showcasing field projects across the region.
- **Contact & Inquiries (`contact.html`)**: Head office location in Djibouti, direct phone lines, email contact, interactive validated inquiry form, and procurement FAQ.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack & Design System

- **HTML5**: Semantic, accessible structure with complete SEO metadata and OpenGraph tags.
- **CSS3 (Vanilla)**:
  - Custom design tokens matching industrial corporate branding (Deep Navy `#0D1B2A`, Warm Gold Accent `#C8973A`, Clean Slate `#F4F6F8`).
  - Fluid typography (`Barlow` and `Inter` via Google Fonts).
  - Fully responsive grid and flexbox layout across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
- **JavaScript (Vanilla)**:
  - Dynamic sticky header on scroll.
  - Mobile slide-out drawer menu.
  - Active page navigation indicators.
  - Interactive project gallery category filters.
  - Animated stats counters via `IntersectionObserver`.
  - Client-side contact form validation with instant user feedback.

## 🚀 Deployment & Local Preview

To preview the website locally:

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/carab6060-maker/amal-website.git
cd amal-website

# Open in browser directly, or serve via any static web server:
npx serve .
# or
python -m http.server 8080
```
