About Our Company

Djibouti-based engineering and infrastructure specialists delivering integrated solutions across the Horn of Africa.

Who We Are

An Engineering Company Built for the Region

Amal Engineering Company is a Djibouti-based engineering and infrastructure firm specialising in construction, water well drilling, hydraulic systems and solar power solutions.

Operating across Djibouti, Ethiopia and Somaliland, Amal Engineering brings together technical expertise and deep regional knowledge to deliver complex, multi-sector infrastructure projects in some of the Horn of Africa's most demanding environments.

From urban construction to rural water access and renewable energy — our integrated service capability allows clients to work with a single, trusted partner across the full scope of their project needs.
