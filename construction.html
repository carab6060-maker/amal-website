Construction & Infrastructure

Construction & Civil Works

Residential, commercial and civil construction delivered with quality materials and professional site management across the Horn of Africa.

What We Build

Full-Spectrum Construction Services

Amal Engineering handles construction projects of all scales — from individual residential builds to commercial developments and large-scale civil infrastructure — applying the same discipline and technical rigour across every scope.

Our construction teams are structured around experienced site engineers and project managers who maintain strict oversight of timelines, quality standards and safety protocols throughout every phase of a build.

Service Areas

Construction Capabilities

Residential Construction

Construction of single and multi-unit residential buildings using quality materials appropriate for regional climate and soil conditions. From foundation laying through to full structural completion.

  • Single and multi-storey residential buildings
  • Reinforced concrete slab and frame structures
  • Foundations, walling, roofing and finishes

Commercial Construction

Purpose-built commercial and institutional facilities designed to meet operational requirements, durability standards and local building codes.

  • Offices, warehouses and commercial buildings
  • Institutional facilities and community buildings
  • Industrial and operational compounds

Engineering Consultancy

Technical advisory services supported by our in-house Design & Drafting Engineer, providing engineering input from concept to construction documentation.

  • Structural design and drafting
  • Technical review and site assessment
  • Construction methodology advice

Renovation & Civil Works

Building renovation, refurbishment and civil infrastructure works — improving existing structures and constructing civil engineering elements to required specifications.

  • Building refurbishment and renovation
  • Roads, drainage and civil infrastructure
  • Earthworks and land preparation
Our Approach

Project-Focused Construction Management

Every construction project at Amal Engineering is managed by an experienced Project Site Engineer supported by the Senior Operations Manager and Design & Drafting Engineer. This structured team approach ensures that technical quality, safety standards and project timelines are maintained throughout the build.

We work closely with clients and relevant authorities at every stage — from design review and site preparation through to structural works, finishing and handover.

  • Dedicated project engineer on every build
  • Durable, quality-specified materials throughout
  • Strict health, safety and environmental standards
  • Continuous client communication and reporting
  • Owned fleet for earthworks and material handling
