Frequently Asked Questions

Working With Amal Engineering

Key information regarding our procurement processes, machinery deployment, and cross-border capabilities.

How quickly can machinery and drilling rigs be mobilized?

With our central fleet yard and maintenance facility based in Djibouti, standard civil machinery and deep rotary drilling rigs can be mobilized within 48 to 72 hours following contract finalization and route planning.

Do you undertake turnkey EPC water supply contracts?

Yes. Amal Engineering delivers end-to-end EPC contracts — covering geophysical survey, rotary drilling, steel/PVC casing, step-drawdown pump testing, solar pumping array installation, elevated reservoir construction, and community distribution networks.

What quality and safety certifications do your field teams follow?

All operations adhere to rigorous HSE management systems aligning with ISO 9001 (Quality), ISO 14001 (Environmental Stewardship), and ISO 45001 (Occupational Health & Safety), with mandatory PPE, daily toolbox briefings, and zero-accident targets.

Can you provide certified equipment rental with operators?

Yes. Our heavy earthmoving machinery, excavators, tipper trucks, and drilling units can be hired with qualified, safety-certified local operators and on-site field maintenance support to guarantee continuous uptime.
