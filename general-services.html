Our Support Services

Keeping Operations Running

Beyond engineering and construction, Amal Engineering provides a range of logistical and facility support services that underpin efficient field operations and client facility management.

Food Supply

Reliable food supply and catering logistics for field camps, project sites and institutional clients. Ensuring that operational teams are properly supported in remote and logistically challenging environments.

  • Field camp food supply and logistics
  • Institutional catering support
  • Supply chain coordination

Cleaning & Janitorial

Professional cleaning and janitorial services for offices, institutional facilities and construction site welfare areas — maintaining hygienic environments for personnel and clients.

  • Office and facility cleaning
  • Construction site welfare areas
  • Institutional and commercial premises

Maintenance Services

Scheduled and reactive maintenance services for facilities, mechanical systems and installed equipment — supporting long-term performance and minimising downtime.

  • Facility and building maintenance
  • Mechanical system maintenance
  • Scheduled preventative maintenance

Repair Services

Rapid response repair services for facilities, infrastructure and equipment. Supported by our Electrical & Mechanical Manager and field maintenance teams.

  • Electrical and mechanical repairs
  • Structural and civil repairs
  • Reactive and emergency response
