About Amal Engineering

A Trusted Partner in Infrastructure Development

Amal Engineering Company is a Djibouti-based engineering and infrastructure firm specialising in construction, water well drilling, hydraulic systems and solar power solutions.

With operational presence across Djibouti, Ethiopia and Somaliland, Amal Engineering brings together technical expertise and regional knowledge to deliver complex, multi-sector infrastructure projects in some of the Horn of Africa's most demanding environments.

Technical Expertise

Skilled engineers and operators across all service lines

Regional Knowledge

Deep understanding of local terrain, logistics and conditions

Capacity to operate concurrent projects across multiple locations

Quality Standards
