Solar Power Solutions

Engineering-grade solar systems for water pumping, residential and institutional applications across the Horn of Africa.

Our Solar Capabilities

Reliable Renewable Energy for the Region

The Horn of Africa benefits from some of the highest solar irradiance levels in the world. Amal Engineering's solar division harnesses this resource to deliver reliable, cost-effective energy systems — engineered for performance and durability in demanding field conditions.

Our approach is engineering-focused: every solar installation is designed, sized and commissioned by our Electrical & Mechanical Manager, ensuring each system meets the specific load, terrain and operational requirements of the installation site.

  • Solar pumping systems for water boreholes
  • Solar panel installation for residential use
  • Renewable energy systems for institutions
  • System sizing, design and commissioning
  • Integration with water well pump systems
Applications

Solar Energy Service Areas

Solar Pumping

Solar-powered pump systems for water wells and boreholes — eliminating fuel dependency and reducing long-term operational costs for water distribution programmes.

Panel Installation

Professional installation of photovoltaic solar panels for residential and small commercial applications, including mounting structure, wiring and inverter systems.

Institutional Energy Systems

Larger-scale renewable energy systems sized for schools, health facilities, administrative buildings and community centres — delivering reliable power where grid access is limited.

Integrated Solutions

Solar + Water: An Integrated System

One of Amal Engineering's most impactful service combinations is the integration of solar power with water well drilling and pump systems. This approach delivers fully off-grid, self-sustaining water supply infrastructure — requiring no fuel, no generator maintenance and no grid connection.

Solar-powered submersible pumps, installed at the wellhead, pump freshwater directly into storage tanks and distribution systems — providing communities, institutions and operations with reliable, renewable water access.
