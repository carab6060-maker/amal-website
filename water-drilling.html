Water Well Drilling & Hydraulic Solutions

End-to-end water infrastructure services — from hydro-geological identification to commissioning and long-term well rehabilitation.

Our Water Expertise

Complete Water Access Solutions

Access to clean, reliable freshwater is one of the most critical infrastructure challenges across the Horn of Africa. Amal Engineering offers a fully integrated water well drilling and hydraulic service — managing every phase of the process from initial site identification through to long-term operational support.

Our drilling teams operate across Djibouti, Ethiopia and Somaliland, equipped with the specialist equipment and technical knowledge to execute both shallow and deep freshwater well projects in varied geological conditions.

  • Hydro-geological site identification and assessment
  • Shallow and deep freshwater well drilling
  • Casing, development and gravel packing
  • Solar, electric and submersible pump installation
  • Water quality testing and yield verification
  • Full system commissioning
  • Water distribution point construction
  • Existing well rehabilitation
Our Methodology

The Drilling Process, Step by Step

A structured, quality-controlled process from the first site visit to final project handover.

01

Site Identification

Hydro-geological survey and site assessment to locate optimal aquifer positions

02

Drilling

Shallow or deep freshwater well drilling using specialist rigs and equipment

03

Casing & Development

Casing installation, gravel packing and well development for optimal performance

04

Pump Installation

Solar, electric or submersible pump selection and installation

05

Testing & Commissioning

Water quality testing, yield verification and full system commissioning

Water Distribution Points

Construction of complete water distribution infrastructure including storage tanks, pipe networks and community access points to ensure equitable water delivery.

Existing Well Rehabilitation

Assessment and rehabilitation of underperforming or non-functional wells, restoring full yield capacity and upgrading pump and distribution infrastructure.

Operational Capacity

Multi-Site Drilling Operations

Amal Engineering's water division is structured to manage multiple concurrent drilling projects across different locations — a critical capability for large-scale water access programmes.

Dedicated Drilling Teams

Experienced drillers, operators and support personnel assigned to each project — maintaining consistent quality and safety standards across all sites.

Coordinated Logistics

Fuel, materials, water carts and equipment are coordinated across all active sites — ensuring no delays in drilling operations.

Structured Supervision

A Drilling Manager provides oversight across all water projects — monitoring progress, safety compliance and technical quality at every site.
